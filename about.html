About MODENA

Identity in the
age of AI.

Intelligent systems are becoming more capable. The infrastructure around them should become more intentional.

Our perspective

Intelligence is
personal to context.

MODENA is an AI identity and context infrastructure initiative built around a simple idea: an agent's usefulness depends not only on what it can do, but on how clearly it can be identified, understood, and authorized.

We see identity as the foundation of trusted interactions, context as the foundation of continuity, and permissions as the foundation of responsible autonomy.

Identity gives intelligence meaning.
